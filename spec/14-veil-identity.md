# 14. Veil Identity Model

Veil Identity is VRP’s non-geographic, non-persistent identity layer.

## Identity Principles

- No stable identifiers  
- No long-term keys  
- No reconstructable fingerprints  
- Identity emerges from behavior, not metadata  

## Identity Components

1. **Entropy Signature**  
   A temporary, non-reversible entropy pattern.

2. **Phase Context**  
   The current behavioral state of the session.

3. **Movement Influence**  
   Subtle variations in user movement that shape routing.

## Identity Guarantees

- Cannot be linked across sessions  
- Cannot be correlated across routes  
- Cannot be reconstructed retroactively  

Veil Identity is presence without exposure.
