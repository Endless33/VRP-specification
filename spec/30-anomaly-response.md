# 30. Anomaly Response

VRP reacts to anomalies through behavioral adaptation.

## Anomaly Types

- entropy spikes  
- movement irregularities  
- timing distortions  
- routing inconsistencies  

## Response Mechanisms

- forced mutation  
- phase shift  
- entropy reset  
- drift redirection  

## Guarantees

Anomalies do not expose identity.  
They trigger motion.
