# 15. Movement Influence Model

Movement is the primary behavioral input of VRP.

## Movement Types

- Micro-movement (fine-grain variation)
- Macro-movement (large-scale change)
- Temporal movement (timing irregularity)
- Behavioral movement (interaction patterns)

## Influence Channels

Movement affects:

- entropy weight  
- routing mutation probability  
- phase transitions  
- drift trajectory  

## Movement Guarantees

- No movement is stored  
- No movement is fingerprinted  
- No movement is reconstructable  

Movement is not telemetry.  
Movement is protection.
