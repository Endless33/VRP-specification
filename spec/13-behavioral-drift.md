# 13. Behavioral Drift Model

Behavioral Drift is the long-term movement of VRP sessions through entropy, phases, and routing states.

## Drift Components

1. **Entropy Drift**  
   Slow accumulation or decay of entropy over time.

2. **Phase Drift**  
   Gradual transitions between behavioral states.

3. **Routing Drift**  
   Long-term mutation patterns influenced by movement.

## Drift Properties

- Non-linear  
- Non-reversible  
- Non-predictable  
- Non-reconstructable  

## Drift Effects

- Session identity becomes fluid  
- Routing paths evolve organically  
- Entropy weight shifts over time  

Behavioral Drift ensures that VRP sessions cannot be profiled or fingerprinted.
